The datasets' license covers their transcriptions; where each page's image comes from, and its
rights, are given below.

Copiste d'un jour (3 pages)

- Repository: https://github.com/banq-dcn/Copiste-d-un-jour
- Authors: Adèle Aubin, Pascale Montmartin
- Citation: see the repository
- Text: volunteers copied French Wikipedia summaries by hand. That text is Wikipedia's contributors',
  under CC BY-SA 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/legalcode), and the tests on
  these pages, which quote it, are under CC BY-SA 4.0 too.
- Consent: each form carries BAnQ's consent text, in which the writer agrees that the page "pourra
  aussi être publié et partagé sous licence CC-BY pour être utilisé librement à des fins non
  commerciales" (may be published and shared under CC BY, to be used freely for non-commercial
  purposes). The dataset itself is published under CC BY 4.0, which has no non-commercial term. We
  use the pages in a benchmark that Datalab, a company, publishes. A hand copy of someone else's text
  carries little copyright of its own, so we believe this use is fair; we say so here rather than
  leave the consent text unmentioned.
